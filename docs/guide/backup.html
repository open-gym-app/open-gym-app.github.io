Backup and restore

Your training history lives on your phone and nowhere else. That keeps it private, and it also means a lost phone is a lost log, unless you keep a backup. Settings › Backup & restore has everything you need.

Export a backup​

Export a backup writes every workout, template, custom exercise, record, measurement, reward and setting to one JSON file. When it is done, you choose what happens to it:

  • Save a copy… to a folder of your choice: your Downloads, an SD card, or a cloud drive app you already use;
  • Share it to another app, such as email or a messenger, to keep it somewhere else.

The file is plain JSON in an open, documented format, so your data is never locked into the app.

The file is not encrypted

A backup holds your whole log, body measurements included. Keep it somewhere you trust. Once you share it, it is in the hands of whatever you shared it with.

Automatic backup​

Switch on Weekly auto-backup and the app writes a backup once a week, while the phone is charging and not being used, typically overnight. It keeps the three most recent and deletes older ones.

These backups are kept in the app's own storage on the phone, so uninstalling the app deletes them. They protect you from mistakes, not from losing the phone. For that, export a backup and save a copy somewhere else now and then.

Restore​

Choose a backup file replaces everything on the phone with the contents of the file.

  • It replaces, it does not merge. Whatever is on the phone now, including anything you logged since the backup was made, is deleted. The confirmation spells out what will go and asks you to type RESTORE.
  • It checks the file first. A damaged file, a file from something else, or a backup from a newer version of the app is refused, and nothing on the phone is changed.
  • Your notification switches and reminder schedule are not in the file: they belong to the phone. Set them again after a restore.

Moving to a new phone​

  1. On the old phone, Export a backup and Save a copy… somewhere the new phone can reach, or Share it to yourself.
  2. Install Titan on the new phone.
  3. Open Settings › Backup & restore › Choose a backup file, and pick the file.

Why not Google backup?​

Android can back apps up to Google Drive on its own. Titan opts out of that on purpose: it would copy your health and training data to a server without you choosing to, and restore it silently on reinstall. With Titan, your data leaves the phone only as a file you exported yourself.
