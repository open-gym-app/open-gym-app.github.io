Brand

The mascot is a dumbbell with a face, standing hands on hips in an orange shirt. It is flat: three colours, no gradients, no shading. Every file on this page is generated by three scripts in this site's repository, so the geometry and the colours live in exactly one place.

Files​

This site serves every file: the SVGs under /brand/svg/, and PNG exports at common sizes under /brand/png/. Use the SVG wherever you can, and a PNG only where an SVG is not accepted.

FileUse it for
titan-mascot.svgThe mascot, with its cream outline. The default choice: it reads on any background.
titan-mascot-no-outline.svgOn light backgrounds, where the cream outline adds nothing.
titan-mascot-on-dark.svgThe mascot on its own dark field: social avatars, slides.
titan-mascot-mono-black.svgOne-colour jobs: print, stamps, embroidery, laser.
titan-head.svgThe head only, for small spaces where the body would turn to mush.
titan-lockup-light.svg, titan-lockup-dark.svgMascot and wordmark, for headers and the README.
titan-banner-dark-640.png, titan-banner-light-640.pngThe lockup centred on an opaque 1280×640 field: GitHub's social preview and other link cards. SVGs alongside.
titan-icon.svg and its layersThe launcher icon on its 108-unit adaptive grid: the mascot's head with the cartoon taken out.
titan-favicon.svg, favicon.icoWeb favicon (16, 32 and 48 px in the .ico).
titan-play-icon.svg, titan-play-icon-512.pngThe Play Store listing icon: square, opaque, no alpha channel.

Colour​

NameHEXRGBRole
Titan orange#FF5A2B255 90 43The shirt, the icon field, the energy marks, the wordmark underline.
Ink#1A1A1A26 26 26Limbs, plates, face, keylines; dark backgrounds.
Cream#FAFAF7250 250 247The dumbbell head, the outline; light backgrounds.

Ink and cream are the app's light-theme ink and paper (core/ui/.../theme/Color.kt). The orange is the mascot's own, between the UI accent's two values (#CC4226 light, #FF6B3D dark): keep it in the mascot and the icon, not in the UI, where the accent is tuned for contrast. For print, get a proof first: screen orange does not survive CMYK unchanged.

Clear space and minimum size​

  • Clear space: leave at least the height of the dumbbell bar, about a fifth of the mascot's height, free on every side. The energy marks above the head count as part of the mascot.
  • Minimum size: the full mascot 48 px or 12 mm tall, the lockup 32 px tall. Below that, use titan-head.svg, and below 24 px the favicon.

Do​

  • Use the outlined mascot on photos and on busy or mid-tone backgrounds.
  • Use the one-colour version whenever only one ink is available, not a tinted full-colour one.
  • Scale it as a whole.

Don't​

  • Recolour the shirt or the head, or add gradients, shadows or glows.
  • Stretch, skew or rotate it, or redraw the face with another expression.
  • Put the no-outline version on a dark background: the limbs disappear.
  • Set "TITAN" in a font next to the mascot. Use the lockup, whose letters are drawn.
  • Crowd it inside its clear space, or place it on orange without the outline.

In the app​

The app draws the mark from these files in its repository, all written by icon.py --app and export.py --app:

In app/src/main/res/:

  • drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml: the adaptive icon's foreground, and the splash screen's icon.
  • drawable/ic_launcher_monochrome.xml: the themed-icon layer for Android 13 and later.
  • values/ic_launcher_background.xml: Titan orange, the icon's field and the splash screen's background.

Elsewhere:

  • core/data/src/main/res/drawable/ic_notification.xml: the 24 dp status-bar glyph on every notification.
  • release/store-assets/titan-play-icon-512.png: the Play Store listing icon.

The adaptive icon itself, mipmap-anydpi/ic_launcher.xml, only names those layers, so it is written by hand.

Changing it​

The scripts are in docs/scripts/brand/ in this site's repository. Edit them, never the generated files: the next run overwrites them. From that directory, with the app checked out next to this repository:

python3 mascot.py                          # static/brand/svg/: mascot, head, lockups, banners
python3 icon.py --app ../../../../titan    # static/brand/svg/: icon, favicon, Play icon; the app's drawables
python3 export.py --app ../../../../titan  # static/brand/png/, favicons, the site's logo; the app's Play icon

export.py needs headless Chromium or Chrome and ImageMagick on the PATH. Point the CHROME environment variable at any other Chromium binary, such as Playwright's. Commit the result in both repositories.
