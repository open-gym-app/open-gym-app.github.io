# Getting started

<AppName /> is a training log for Android. It runs entirely on your phone: there is no
account to create and nothing to sign in to. Install it, answer three questions, and your
first workout is one tap away.

## Install

<AppName /> is not on Google Play yet. It is being prepared for its first release; this page
will link to the listing as soon as it is live. Until then you can
[build it from source](developers/contributing#build-the-app).

You need Android 8.0 or newer. The app is designed for phones held upright.

## The first launch

The first time you open the app it asks three questions. Each one takes a tap, you can skip
any of them, and every answer can be changed later in <kbd>Settings</kbd>.

| Question | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **What are you training for?** Strength, hypertrophy, endurance, or your own range | The rep range the app aims your suggested loads at: 3–6 for strength, 8–12 for hypertrophy, 15–20 for endurance, or the range you type. |
| **How long have you been training?** Beginner, intermediate or advanced | How quickly suggested loads go up. A beginner is offered more weight the first time every set reaches the top of the range; everyone else after two sessions in a row. |
| **Kilos or pounds?** | How weights and distances are shown. Every set is stored the same way underneath, so switching later changes nothing you have logged. |

## Find your way around

The app has four tabs, plus a gear for settings in the top bar.

- **Home** shows your level, the current week, your quests and a button to start training.
- **Sessions** holds your history, your [templates](guide/templates) and a training calendar.
- **Library** is the [exercise library](guide/library): 873 exercises, plus your own.
- **Stats** is where [your progress](guide/progress) lives: volume, records, charts, body
  measurements and the [quest log](guide/quests).

## Your first workout

Tap **Start empty session** on Home, add an exercise from the library, and log your first
set. [Logging a workout](guide/logging) walks through the rest.
