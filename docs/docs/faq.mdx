# Frequently asked questions

### Is it really free?

Yes. There is no trial, no subscription and nothing to unlock. <AppName /> is open source under
the GNU GPL v3, made by volunteers.

### Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. You open the app and start logging.

### Does it work offline?

It only works offline. The app does not ask Android for internet access, so it never connects
to anything. Everything works the same in a basement gym with no signal.

### Where is my data?

On your phone, in the app's private storage. Nobody else has a copy: not us, not Google, not
an analytics company. The [privacy policy](/privacy) has the details.

### What happens if I lose my phone?

Your log goes with it, unless you have a backup somewhere else. Export one now and then and
save a copy off the phone. See [Backup and restore](guide/backup).

### Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes: export a backup on the old phone, and restore it on the new one.
[Here is how](guide/backup#moving-to-a-new-phone).

### Can I get my data out to use elsewhere?

Yes. The backup is a plain JSON file in a [documented format](developers/export-format): one
array per table, with readable names. A spreadsheet or a script can read it.

### Is there an iPhone version?

No. <AppName /> is built for Android only.

### Does it sync with Health Connect, Google Fit or a watch?

Not yet. A Health Connect sync is planned: see the [roadmap](roadmap).

### Can I log cardio?

Yes. The library includes cardio exercises, and records track the longest distance and best
pace for them. More outdoor cardio and swimming exercises are on the way.

### How is my one-rep max estimated?

From your best set, with the Epley formula by default, or Brzycki if you prefer. Change it in
<kbd>Settings</kbd> › <kbd>1RM formula</kbd>.

### I found a bug, or I have an idea.

<NewIssueLink>Open an issue</NewIssueLink> on GitHub. Say what you did, what you expected,
and what happened instead. Screenshots help.

### Can I help?

Gladly. Code, translations, testing on your phone, or just telling us what is confusing: see
[Contributing](developers/contributing).
