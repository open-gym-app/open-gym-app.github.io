# Templates

A template is a workout you plan to repeat: its exercises in order, with the sets, reps and
weights you mean to do. Starting one fills the workout in for you, so a push day takes one tap
instead of six trips to the library.

## Make one

There are two ways.

- **From a workout you just finished.** On the summary, tap **Save as template**. The template
  gets the exercises you performed, in order, with the sets, reps and weights you hit as its
  defaults.
- **From scratch.** In **Sessions** › **Templates**, tap **New**. Give it a name and a
  category, add exercises, and plan each set.

Each template shows how many exercises it has, roughly how long it takes, and its category:
strength, hypertrophy, endurance, mobility or general.

## Use one

From **Home**, tap **Start from template** and pick it. The workout opens with every set
planned; change anything on the day and the template itself stays as it was.

## Keep them tidy

From a template's menu you can **rename** it, **duplicate** it (handy for a heavy and a light
version of the same day) or **delete** it. Deleting a template never touches the workouts you
already logged from it; they stay in your history.

If an exercise in a template is later deleted from your library, the template shows it as
removed, and starting the template leaves it out.
