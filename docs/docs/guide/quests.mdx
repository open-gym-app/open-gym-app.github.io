# XP, quests and rewards

Showing up is most of training, so <AppName /> turns it into a small game. None of it gets in
the way of logging, and none of it costs anything. **Stats** › **Quest log** has the whole
picture.

## XP and levels

Every finished workout earns experience points:

| For | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Finishing the session | 25 |
| Each completed set | 5 |
| Each personal record | 50 |
| Each achievement unlocked | 100 |

Every 100 XP is a level. Levels come with titles as you climb, from **Newcomer** through
**Regular**, **Iron Disciple**, **Steel Forged**, **Relentless** and **Unbreakable** to
**Legend**. The workout summary shows what you earned and why, and celebrates a level-up.

## Quests

Quests are goals that reset, so there is always one in reach. Each is worth XP when you clear
it.

| This week | This month |
| --- | --- |
| Train three times | Train twelve times |
| Train five times | Break five personal records |
| Break a personal record | Move 80 tonnes |
| Move 20 tonnes | |

"Move 20 tonnes" means total volume: every set's weight × reps, added up. Home shows how far
along you are.

## Streaks, and saving one

Your streak counts the days in a row you trained. Real life gets in the way, so you have three
**streak protections** a month: once a streak is at least three days long, missing a single
day uses a protection instead of breaking it. The summary tells you when one was spent, and
the quest log shows how many you have left this month.

## Loot

A finished workout can drop a collectible. Most are common; some take months to find.

| Rarity | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Chalk block, Spare collar, Worn wristband |
| Uncommon | Lifting strap, Steel shaker |
| Rare | Pet egg |
| Epic | Golden dumbbell |
| Legendary | Iron crown |

Your recent finds are in the quest log. Collectibles are just that for now: things to collect.

## Achievements

Achievements are permanent: unlike quests, they never reset. Each is struck as a medal the day
you earn it.

- First workout logged
- Ten workouts in
- One hundred workouts
- A week-long streak
- A month-long streak
- First personal record

## Turning it down

Level-ups, completed quests and loot can also arrive as notifications. If you would rather
not hear about them, turn off <kbd>Settings</kbd> › <kbd>Streaks and rewards</kbd>.
