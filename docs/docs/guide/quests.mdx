# XP, quests and rewards

Showing up is most of training, so <AppName /> turns it into a small game. None of it gets in
the way of logging, and none of it costs anything. **Stats** › **Quest log** has the whole
picture.

## XP and levels

Every finished workout earns experience points:

| For | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Finishing a session with at least three working sets | 50 |
| Each working set, up to 20 a session | 5 |
| Each personal record beaten, up to five a session | 10 |
| Each achievement unlocked | 100 |

Only work pays. Warm-up sets and sets ticked with nothing on them earn nothing, and neither does
the first figure you ever log for an exercise: a record has to beat an earlier one.

The first levels come quickly and each one costs a little more than the last: level 2 takes 60 XP
in all, level 10 takes 1,620, and level 100 about a year of training four times a week. Levels come
with titles as you climb:

| Level | Title |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Newcomer |
| 10 | Regular |
| 20 | Iron Disciple |
| 40 | Steel Forged |
| 60 | Relentless |
| 80 | Unbreakable |
| 100 | Legend |

The workout summary shows what you earned and why, and celebrates a level-up.

## Quests

Quests are goals that reset, so there is always one in reach. Each is worth XP when you clear
it.

| This week | XP | This month | XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Train three times | 60 | Train twelve times | 250 |
| Train five times | 100 | Break five personal records | 300 |
| Break a personal record | 80 | Move 80 tonnes | 280 |
| Move 20 tonnes | 70 | | |

"Move 20 tonnes" means total volume: every set's weight × reps, added up. Home shows how far
along you are.

## Streaks, and saving one

Your streak counts the **weeks** in a row you met your weekly goal. The goal is four workouts a
week until you change it, from one to seven, in <kbd>Settings</kbd> › <kbd>Weekly goal</kbd>. Rest
days never break a streak, and neither does the week you are in: a week is only judged once it is
over. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

Real life gets in the way, so you have one **streak protection** a month: once a streak is at least
two weeks long, a single week that falls short uses the protection instead of breaking it. Two
short weeks in a row do break it. The summary tells you when a protection was spent, and the quest
log shows whether you have one left this month.

## Loot

A finished workout can drop a collectible. Most are common; some take months to find.

| Rarity | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Chalk block, Spare collar, Worn wristband |
| Uncommon | Lifting strap, Steel shaker |
| Rare | Pet egg |
| Epic | Golden dumbbell |
| Legendary | Iron crown |

Your recent finds are in the quest log. Collectibles are just that for now: things to collect.

## Achievements

Achievements are permanent: unlike quests, they never reset. Each is struck as a medal the day
you earn it.

- First workout logged
- Ten workouts in
- One hundred workouts
- A month-long streak (four weeks)
- A three-month streak (twelve weeks)
- First personal record

## After an import

A [history imported from another app](import) earns XP, quests, achievements and loot by the same
rules, workout by workout, in the order you trained. Imported weeks older than your current streak do not
extend it, and an achievement you already have keeps its date.

## Turning it down

Level-ups, completed quests and loot can also arrive as notifications. If you would rather
not hear about them, turn off <kbd>Settings</kbd> › <kbd>Streaks and rewards</kbd>.
