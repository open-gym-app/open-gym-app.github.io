# Settings

Tap the gear at the top of any tab. Every setting takes effect straight away.

<Screen name="settings" alt="Settings: appearance, workout, data and privacy, notifications and about." caption="Settings." />

## Appearance

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Theme** | Light, dark, or **Auto** to follow your phone. |
| **Units** | Metric (kg, cm, km) or imperial (lb, in, mi). Your log is stored the same way either way, so switching back and forth changes nothing you have recorded. |
| **Font scale** | Follows your phone's text size. Change it in Android's display settings; the app is built to stay readable at twice the default size. |

The app's language follows your phone's. On Android 13 and newer you can also pick a language
for <AppName /> alone, in Android's settings under *Apps* › <AppName /> › *Language*.

## Workout

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Default rest timer** | How long you rest between sets, for exercises that have no rest time of their own. |
| **Plate calculator bar** | The weight of the empty bar: 20, 15 or 10 kg (45, 35 or 25 lb), or your own. |
| **Training goal** | Strength (3–6 reps), hypertrophy (8–12), endurance (15–20) or custom. Sets the rep range your [next targets](logging#the-next-target) aim for. With **Custom** you choose the range and how much weight to add each time. |
| **Fitness level** | Beginner, intermediate or advanced. Paces how quickly suggested weights go up. |
| **1RM formula** | Epley or Brzycki, for the estimated one-rep max in [your records](progress#personal-records). |
| **Warm-up suggestions** | Whether a workout offers a [warm-up and a cool-down](logging#warm-up-and-cool-down). |

## Data & privacy

- **Backup & restore** opens [the backup screen](backup).
- **Health Connect** is *coming later* and does nothing yet.
- **Delete all data** erases everything, described below.

## Notifications

One switch per kind of notification: **Workout reminders** (with [its own
screen](reminders)), **Rest timer alerts**, **Unfinished sessions**, and **Streaks and
rewards**. If Android is blocking the app's notifications, this section says so and links to
the system setting.

## About

The app version, a link to the source code, and **Open source licences**: every library the
app is built on, with its licence.

## Delete all data

<kbd>Settings</kbd> › <kbd>Delete all data</kbd> erases every workout, template, custom
exercise, record, measurement and reward from the phone, and puts every setting back to its
default. The confirmation lists what will go and asks you to type **DELETE**.

There is no undo and no copy anywhere else: that is the point. If you might want your history
back, [export a backup](backup) first.
