# Body measurements

**Stats** › **Body measurements** keeps a log of your body next to the log of your training.

<Screen name="body" alt="Body over 90 days: 78.4 kg body weight trending down 1.6 kg, chest, waist, arm and thigh measurements, 14.2% body fat, 67.27 kg lean mass, and the progress photos below." caption="Body measurements over 90 days." />

Tap **+** to add an entry. Every field is optional, so a single number is a fine start:

- body weight;
- body fat percentage;
- neck, shoulders, chest, waist, hips, arm, forearm, thigh and calf, by tape measure. There is one
  figure per limb, so measure the same side each time;
- a note.

The screen charts your body weight over the window you pick, shows the latest of each
measurement on a body diagram, and works out your **lean mass** when it has both a weight and
a body fat reading. Below that is every entry you have made; tap one to edit it, or
swipe it left to delete it. A deleted entry can be brought back with **Undo**.

Units follow your setting: kilograms and centimetres, or pounds and inches.

## Progress photos

**Progress photos**, on the same screen, keeps pictures of how you change. Add one with **Take a
photo** or **Choose from gallery**; a photo from the gallery is filed on the day it was taken. Open
one and tap **Compare** to put it next to another.

Photos are kept more strictly than anything else in the app:

- They are stored in the app's private storage, not in your gallery, so no other app can read them.
- **They are never in a backup, never in the cloud**, and they do not move to a new phone. Deleting
  one deletes the only copy the app has.
- The app stores its own copy without the metadata a camera adds, such as where the photo was taken.
- [Delete all data](settings#delete-all-data) deletes them. Restoring a backup does not touch them.

## Your body data stays private

Weight, body fat and measurements are health data, and they get the same treatment as the rest
of your log: they are stored on your phone and nowhere else. The app has no way to send them
anywhere. The only way they leave the phone is inside a [backup file you export
yourself](backup), and progress photos are not even in that. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).
