# Body measurements

**Stats** › **Body measurements** keeps a log of your body next to the log of your training.

<Screen name="body" alt="Body over 90 days: 78.4 kg body weight trending down 1.6 kg, chest, waist, arm and thigh measurements, 14.2% body fat and 67.27 kg lean mass." caption="Body measurements over 90 days." />

Tap **+** to add an entry. Every field is optional, so a single number is a fine start:

- body weight;
- body fat percentage;
- chest, waist, arm and thigh, by tape measure;
- a note.

The screen charts your body weight over the window you pick, shows the latest of each
measurement on a body diagram, and works out your **lean mass** when it has both a weight and
a body fat reading. Below that is every entry you have made; tap one to edit or delete it.

Units follow your setting: kilograms and centimetres, or pounds and inches.

## Your body data stays private

Weight, body fat and measurements are health data, and they get the same treatment as the rest
of your log: they are stored on your phone and nowhere else. The app has no way to send them
anywhere. The only way they leave the phone is inside a [backup file you export
yourself](backup). See the [privacy policy](/privacy).
