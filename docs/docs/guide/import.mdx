# Import from another app

Coming from another tracker? Bring your history with you. <AppName /> reads the files that
Strong, Hevy, FitNotes, JEFIT, Fitbod and StrongLifts export, and a [plain CSV](import-csv) you
can make from any other app or spreadsheet.

Open it from <kbd>Settings</kbd> › <kbd>Import from another app</kbd>, or tap **Import my
history** on the last screen of the [first launch](../intro#the-first-launch).

:::tip[An import adds, it never replaces]
Everything already on your phone stays. That is the opposite of
[restoring a backup](backup#restore), which replaces it all.
:::

## How it works

1. **Export a file** from your old app and save it on the phone, or somewhere the phone can
   reach. The steps for each app are [below](#export-from-your-app).
2. In <AppName />, pick the app, tap **Choose file** and select the file.
3. **Wait, or leave.** The import runs in the background with a progress notification, so you
   can leave the screen. A long history can take a few minutes.
4. **Read the report**: what was added, which exercises were matched or created, what was left
   out, and what your history earned.

If a workout is in progress, the app asks first: saving sets has to wait while an import runs.

## Export from your app

Menus in other apps change; these are the steps the app itself shows. Some apps export more than
one file: import each on its own.

| App | How to export | What comes across | What does not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strong** | *Settings* › *Export Strong Data* (*Export Data* on Android) | Workouts, with their sets, rests and notes | Templates, measurements and supersets, which the export does not hold |
| **Hevy** | *Profile* › *Settings* › *Export & Import Data*, then *Export Workouts*, and *Export Measurements* for body measurements | Workouts, supersets and body measurements. The workouts export from Hevy's website works too | Routines and progress photos, which are in neither file |
| **FitNotes** | *Settings* › *Spreadsheet Export*, for your workouts and, if you use it, your body tracker | Workouts and body tracker entries | Routines, workout comments and times of day. A `.fitnotes` backup file cannot be imported |
| **JEFIT** | *Settings* › *Export Data*: one file for the whole account | Workouts, routines (as [templates](templates)), body stats and custom exercises | Program schedules |
| **Fitbod** | *Log* tab › settings menu › *Export Workout Data*. The Android app has no export: ask Fitbod's support for your workout data | Workouts and their sets | Recommendations and muscle recovery |
| **StrongLifts** | *Settings* › *Export Data*. If it is behind the paywall, use *Contact Support* and they email you the same file | Workouts and body weight | The program's next weights: <AppName /> [suggests its own](logging#the-next-target) |
| **Plain CSV** | Any spreadsheet, or a file converted from another app. See [the format](import-csv) | Workouts, or body measurements | Templates and progress photos |

## What happens to your data

- **Exercises are matched by name**, and by equipment when the file says what it was, so your
  imported bench press and the one you log tomorrow share one set of records. Anything that
  matches nothing becomes a [custom exercise](library#your-own-exercises), which the report
  suggests you review.
- **Records are worked out again** from your sets, with the same rules as a workout you log
  here. They can differ from what the other app showed.
- **XP, achievements, quests and loot are earned too**, workout by workout, in the order you
  trained.
- **Your streak only moves forward.** Imported weeks older than your current streak do not
  extend it, and an achievement you already have keeps the date you first earned it.
- **A workout without a duration** is given two minutes per set.
- **Assisted exercises keep their reps, not the assistance.** There is no field for the help a
  machine or a band gives.

## Importing the same file twice

That is safe. A workout that started at the same moment as one already on the phone is the same
workout, and it is skipped. So is a body measurement taken at the same moment, or a template of
the same name that plans the same exercises. You can export again later and import only what is
new.

## Cancelling, and when something goes wrong

Your history goes in all at once or not at all. If you cancel while workouts are being saved, or
the import fails, nothing is imported and the phone is as it was.

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **This file is not a … export** | You picked the wrong app or the wrong file. Check both. |
| **It could not be read** | The file is damaged. Export it again. |
| **Too large**, or **too many sets** | One import reads a file up to 32 MB, and up to 500,000 sets. |
| **It holds nothing … can import** | The file was read, but there was nothing to add from it. |
| **Your phone refused to save** | Usually full storage. Free some space and try again. |
| **The import did not finish** | The app was closed part way. Run it again with the same file: it adds only what is missing. |

Single sets or workouts the app cannot use, such as a thousand-tonne squat or a workout dated in
the future, are skipped and counted in the report.

## It stays on your phone

The app reads the file you picked and nothing else. It does not sign in to your old app, and like
everything in <AppName /> it needs no internet connection.
