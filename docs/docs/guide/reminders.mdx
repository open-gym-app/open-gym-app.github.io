# Reminders and notifications

## Workout reminders

<Screen name="reminder" alt="Workout reminders at 6:00 PM on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, next on Monday." caption="Settings › Workout reminders." />

<kbd>Settings</kbd> › <kbd>Workout reminders</kbd> sets a nudge on the days you plan to
train. Switch on **Remind me to train**, pick the days and the time, and the screen tells you
when the next one is due. If you have already started a workout that day, the reminder asks
you to finish it instead.

Reminders survive a restart of the phone. If Android does not let the app use exact alarms,
the screen says so: the reminder still comes, possibly a few minutes late.

## Every notification the app can send

All of them are created on your phone. None comes from a server, because there is no server.

| Notification | When | Switch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time to train** | On your reminder days, at your reminder time. | Workout reminders |
| **Rest is over** | When a rest timer ends while the app is in the background, with a buzz. It has a **Skip rest** button while the rest is running. | Rest timer alerts |
| **Workout still open** | A silent note when a workout has been left open with nothing logged. | Unfinished sessions |
| **Level, quest or loot** | After a workout that levels you up, clears a quest, saves your streak or drops loot. | Streaks and rewards |

The switches are in <kbd>Settings</kbd> › *Notifications*. Each kind is also its own channel
in Android's notification settings, so you can change its sound or importance there.

## The permission

On Android 13 and newer, apps need permission to post notifications. <AppName /> does not ask
when you open it. It asks the first time you switch a notification on, so you know what you
are agreeing to. If you said no, the Notifications section in Settings says notifications are
blocked and takes you to the system setting.
