# Accessibility and languages

A training log is used one-handed, between sets, with sweaty hands and a racing pulse. That is
reason enough to build it for everyone, and <AppName /> is checked against these promises on
every build, not once before a release.

## What you can count on

- **TalkBack reads everything.** Every control has a name, and it contains the words drawn on
  it, so Voice Access users can say what they see. Charts and the body diagram are read as one
  sentence each, not point by point.
- **Things that change are announced.** The rest countdown, the set counter and export
  progress are spoken as they change, without talking over you.
- **Large text works.** Every screen is checked at twice the default font size.
- **Controls are big enough.** Everything you can tap is at least 48 × 48 dp.
- **Contrast.** Both themes meet WCAG AA contrast, checked automatically.

If something in the app does not hold up to this, it is a bug: please
<NewIssueLink>report it</NewIssueLink>.

## Languages

The app is available in:

- English
- French
- Spanish
- Brazilian Portuguese

It follows your phone's language. On Android 13 and newer you can choose a language for
<AppName /> alone in Android's settings. Numbers, dates and units are written the way your
language writes them: `1 234,5 kg` in French, `1,234.5 kg` in English.

Translations use the words people say at the gym, not a dictionary's: *série* and *séance* in
French, *serie* and *plantilla* in Spanish, *treino* and *carga* in Portuguese. Portuguese
speakers in Portugal get the Brazilian translation for now. If you would like to help with
another language, see [Translating](../developers/translating).

## Units and themes

Pick kilograms or pounds, light or dark or automatic, in [Settings](settings). Switching units
never changes a logged set: every weight is stored exactly and only displayed differently.
