# Roadmap

What is planned, roughly in order. Nothing here is a promise of a date: <AppName /> is built
by volunteers, and a feature ships when it is ready. Every item has an issue on GitHub where
you can follow it or weigh in.

## Getting to the first release

The app is feature-complete for its first version. What is left is release work:

- the Play Store listing, in the four languages the app speaks (<Issue n={46} />);
- privacy and store declarations (<Issue n={45} />), a security audit (<Issue n={48} />),
  and a staged rollout (<Issue n={49} />);
- a set of ready-made workout templates to start from (<Issue n={213} />);
- swimming and outdoor cardio in the exercise library (<Issue n={212} />).

## After that

These already have a row in Settings, greyed out and marked *Soon*, so the app's layout does
not shift when they arrive.

### Health Connect

Sync body weight and body fat with Android's Health Connect, both ways (<Issue n={54} />). It
will be off until you turn it on, and the [privacy policy](/privacy) will be updated before it
ships.

### Marketplace

Share your templates and challenges with other people, and try theirs (<Issue n={52} />).

### Gym map

Find gyms and equipment near you (<Issue n={50} />).

Both would need the app to go online for the first time. For an app whose first promise is
that nothing leaves the phone, that is a big change, and one to talk through in their issues
before any of it is built.
