# <AppName /> privacy policy

*Effective 27 September 2026. Also available in [français](pathname:///fr/privacy), [español](pathname:///es/privacy) and [português (Brasil)](pathname:///pt-BR/privacy).*

<AppName /> is a workout tracker for Android. It is free, open source, and made by volunteers.
This policy covers the <AppName /> app (<AndroidPackage />) as published on Google Play and built
from the source repository.

## The short version

- <AppName /> **collects nothing**. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
  advertising.
- The app **cannot connect to the internet**. It does not request Android's `INTERNET`
  permission, so the system does not let it open a network connection.
- Everything you log stays **on your phone**, in the app's private storage.
- Your data leaves the phone only when **you** export it, to a place **you** choose.
- You can **delete everything** at any time from the app.

## What the app stores, and where

<AppName /> keeps the following on your device so that it can work as a training log:

| What | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Workouts | sessions, exercises, sets, weights, reps, effort (RPE), rest times, notes |
| Your exercise library and templates | custom exercises, notes on exercises, saved workout templates |
| Body data | body weight, body fat percentage, body measurements (waist, arms, and so on) |
| Progress | personal records, experience points, level, streaks, quests, achievements, collected items |
| Settings | units, theme, training goal, fitness level, rest timer, reminder days and time, bar and plates |

This is kept in the app's private database and settings files, which Android lets no other
app read.

If you turn on **Weekly auto-backup** (Settings › Backup & restore), the app also writes a
backup file once a week into its own folder on the device's shared storage
(<AndroidPackage prefix="Android/data/" suffix="/files/backups" />). It keeps the three most recent and deletes
older ones. These files stay on the phone, and Android deletes them when you uninstall the
app.

### Health data

Body weight, body fat and body measurements are health data. <AppName /> uses them for one
purpose only: to show you your own entries and trends. They are never transmitted, sold,
shared, or used for advertising. <AppName /> does not read from or write to Health Connect, or
any other health service. If a future version adds a Health Connect sync, this policy will
be updated before that version is released, and the sync will be off until you turn it on.

## What the app does not do

- **No data collection.** Neither the <AppName /> project nor anyone else receives your data.
  We have no server that could receive it.
- **No network access.** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission. It does
  request `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, added by the Android job scheduler library it uses. That
  permission only lets it ask whether the phone is online; it cannot send or receive
  anything.
- **No third-party SDKs that collect data.** No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or
  tracking libraries are included. The full list of libraries is in the app, under
  Settings › About › Open source licences.
- **No cloud backup.** The app opts its data out of Android's automatic backup to Google
  Drive and of device-to-device transfer, so your training history is not copied off the
  phone behind your back.
- **No account and no advertising identifier.** The app does not know who you are.

## When your data leaves the phone

Only when you ask it to. **Export a backup** (Settings › Backup & restore) writes all of your
data, including your body data, to a JSON file. You then choose to save a copy somewhere or
share it with another app. From that moment the copy is handled by whatever you sent it to,
under that service's own terms. The file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere you trust.

**Restore** reads a backup file you pick and replaces the data on the phone with it. Nothing
is uploaded.

## Notifications

Workout reminders, the rest-timer alert, the unfinished-workout note, and level and reward
notifications are all created on the phone. None is sent from a server. You can turn each
one off in Settings › Notifications or in Android's settings.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why the app asks for it |
| --- | --- |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | To show the notifications above. Android 13+ asks you first. |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` (Android 12 and below) / `USE_EXACT_ALARM` | To end a rest timer on the second, and to fire a workout reminder at the time you set. |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | To schedule your workout reminders and the weekly backup again after the phone restarts. |
| `VIBRATE` | To buzz when a rest period ends. |
| `WAKE_LOCK`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Added by Android's job scheduler library (WorkManager), which runs the weekly backup. None of them grants network access. |
| <AndroidPackage suffix=".DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION" /> | Added by the AndroidX core library. It keeps other apps from sending the app internal messages. |

## Deleting your data

- **Settings › Delete all data** erases everything the app has stored and resets every
  setting. It cannot be undone.
- **Uninstalling the app** deletes everything too, including the automatic backups.
- **Exported files** you saved or shared are outside the app. Delete them wherever you put
  them.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete, and no request to
send.

## Children

from anyone, of any age.
