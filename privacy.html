Titan privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026. Also available in français, español and português (Brasil).

Titan is a workout tracker for Android. It is free, open source, and made by volunteers. This policy covers the Titan app (com.titan.app) as published on Google Play and built from the source repository.

The short version​

  • Titan collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.
  • The app cannot connect to the internet. It does not request Android's INTERNET permission, so the system does not let it open a network connection.
  • Everything you log stays on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Your data leaves the phone only when you export it, to a place you choose.
  • You can delete everything at any time from the app.

What the app stores, and where​

Titan keeps the following on your device so that it can work as a training log:

WhatExamples
Workoutssessions, exercises, sets, weights, reps, effort (RPE), rest times, notes
Your exercise library and templatescustom exercises, notes on exercises, saved workout templates
Body databody weight, body fat percentage, body measurements (waist, arms, and so on)
Progresspersonal records, experience points, level, streaks, quests, achievements, collected items
Settingsunits, theme, training goal, fitness level, rest timer, reminder days and time, bar and plates

This is kept in the app's private database and settings files, which Android lets no other app read.

If you turn on Weekly auto-backup (Settings › Backup & restore), the app also writes a backup file once a week into its own folder on the device's shared storage (Android/data/com.titan.app/files/backups). It keeps the three most recent and deletes older ones. These files stay on the phone, and Android deletes them when you uninstall the app.

Health data​

Body weight, body fat and body measurements are health data. Titan uses them for one purpose only: to show you your own entries and trends. They are never transmitted, sold, shared, or used for advertising. Titan does not read from or write to Health Connect, or any other health service. If a future version adds a Health Connect sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the sync will be off until you turn it on.

What the app does not do​

  • No data collection. Neither the Titan project nor anyone else receives your data. We have no server that could receive it.
  • No network access. The app does not request the INTERNET permission. It does request ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, added by the Android job scheduler library it uses. That permission only lets it ask whether the phone is online; it cannot send or receive anything.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data. No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries are included. The full list of libraries is in the app, under Settings › About › Open source licences.
  • No cloud backup. The app opts its data out of Android's automatic backup to Google Drive and of device-to-device transfer, so your training history is not copied off the phone behind your back.
  • No account and no advertising identifier. The app does not know who you are.

When your data leaves the phone​

Only when you ask it to. Export a backup (Settings › Backup & restore) writes all of your data, including your body data, to a JSON file. You then choose to save a copy somewhere or share it with another app. From that moment the copy is handled by whatever you sent it to, under that service's own terms. The file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere you trust.

Restore reads a backup file you pick and replaces the data on the phone with it. Nothing is uploaded.

Notifications​

Workout reminders, the rest-timer alert, the unfinished-workout note, and level and reward notifications are all created on the phone. None is sent from a server. You can turn each one off in Settings › Notifications or in Android's settings.

Permissions​

PermissionWhy the app asks for it
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo show the notifications above. Android 13+ asks you first.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM (Android 12 and below) / USE_EXACT_ALARMTo end a rest timer on the second, and to fire a workout reminder at the time you set.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDTo schedule your workout reminders and the weekly backup again after the phone restarts.
VIBRATETo buzz when a rest period ends.
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEAdded by Android's job scheduler library (WorkManager), which runs the weekly backup. None of them grants network access.
com.titan.app.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSIONAdded by the AndroidX core library. It keeps other apps from sending the app internal messages.

Deleting your data​

  • Settings › Delete all data erases everything the app has stored and resets every setting. It cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything too, including the automatic backups.
  • Exported files you saved or shared are outside the app. Delete them wherever you put them.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete, and no request to send.

Children​

Titan is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy​

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date. Every past version is kept in the project's public source history. A change that makes the app collect or send data would be announced in the release notes of the version that makes it, and would require your consent in the app.

Contact​

Titan is maintained in the open. For any question about this policy or your privacy, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
