XP, quests and rewards

Showing up is most of training, so Titan turns it into a small game. None of it gets in the way of logging, and none of it costs anything. Stats › Quest log has the whole picture.

XP and levels​

Every finished workout earns experience points:

ForXP
Finishing the session25
Each completed set5
Each personal record50
Each achievement unlocked100

Every 100 XP is a level. Levels come with titles as you climb, from Newcomer through Regular, Iron Disciple, Steel Forged, Relentless and Unbreakable to Legend. The workout summary shows what you earned and why, and celebrates a level-up.

Quests​

Quests are goals that reset, so there is always one in reach. Each is worth XP when you clear it.

This weekThis month
Train three timesTrain twelve times
Train five timesBreak five personal records
Break a personal recordMove 80 tonnes
Move 20 tonnes

"Move 20 tonnes" means total volume: every set's weight × reps, added up. Home shows how far along you are.

Streaks, and saving one​

Your streak counts the days in a row you trained. Real life gets in the way, so you have three streak protections a month: once a streak is at least three days long, missing a single day uses a protection instead of breaking it. The summary tells you when one was spent, and the quest log shows how many you have left this month.

Loot​

A finished workout can drop a collectible. Most are common; some take months to find.

RarityItems
CommonChalk block, Spare collar, Worn wristband
UncommonLifting strap, Steel shaker
RarePet egg
EpicGolden dumbbell
LegendaryIron crown

Your recent finds are in the quest log. Collectibles are just that for now: things to collect.

Achievements​

Achievements are permanent: unlike quests, they never reset. Each is struck as a medal the day you earn it.

  • First workout logged
  • Ten workouts in
  • One hundred workouts
  • A week-long streak
  • A month-long streak
  • First personal record

Turning it down​

Level-ups, completed quests and loot can also arrive as notifications. If you would rather not hear about them, turn off Settings › Streaks and rewards.
