Log the work. Watch it add up.

Titan is a training log for Android. Sets, records, progress and a little play, all of it on your phone and none of it anywhere else.

Coming soon to Google PlayRead the source

Free. No account. No ads. Android 8 and up.

Nothing leaves your phone

The app does not even ask Android for internet access. Your training history lives on the device, and you decide if it ever goes anywhere else.

No account, no ads

Open it and start logging. There is nobody to sign up with, nothing to subscribe to and nothing to sell you.

Open source

Licensed under the GPL v3. Every line is on GitHub: read it, build it, improve it.

Built for the minute between sets

Weight, reps and effort, one tap per set. Everything else stays out of the way until you need it.

A target for every set
What you did last time sits next to each set, with the load to try next, paced to your goal and experience. Nothing is logged until you accept it.
A rest timer that keeps time
It starts when you tick a set, and buzzes on the second even with the screen off.
Every kind of set
Warm-ups, drop sets, sets to failure and supersets, with RPE when you want it.
A plate calculator for your rack
Tell it which plates your gym has and it loads the bar for you, per side.
How logging works

See every kilo add up

Finish a workout and the numbers are already there: volume, records and what you have been training, over a week or over years.

Personal records, found for you
Heaviest set, most reps, best volume and an estimated one-rep max, for every exercise.
Charts for every lift
From the last seven days to all time, with each session one tap away.
The muscles you actually train
A body map of the sets each muscle got, and a calendar shaded by the day's volume.
Your body, too
Body weight, body fat and tape measurements, charted next to your training.
Everything it tracks

Something to come back for

Consistency is the hard part, so the app makes a small game of it. It never gets in the way of the log.

XP and levels
Every set, session and record earns XP, from Newcomer all the way to Legend.
Weekly and monthly quests
Train three times, break a record, move twenty tonnes. They reset, so there is always another.
Streaks that forgive a missed day
Three saves a month keep a streak alive through one missed day.
Loot and medals
Finished sessions can drop anything from a chalk block to an iron crown, and achievements are yours for good.
How XP and quests work

Start from what you know

A library of 873 exercises ships inside the app, with the muscles each one works and how to do it. Add your own in seconds.

Search and filter
By name, muscle or equipment, from barbell to resistance band.
Templates
Build one, or save a finished workout as one, and start it again in one tap.
Notes where you need them
A seat setting on an exercise, how a session felt. They are there next time.
Exercises and templates

Your data, on your terms

Years of training deserve better than a server you do not control. It all stays on your phone, and you can take it with you whenever you like.

Export and restore
Every workout, template, record and setting in one open JSON file, and a new phone set up from it in seconds.
Weekly backup, on its own
While the phone charges overnight. The three latest are kept.
Delete means delete
One setting erases everything, for good. There is no copy anywhere else to forget about.
Read the privacy policy

Made for every hand

Every screen is checked with TalkBack and at twice the font size before it ships.

Accessible by default
Charts read as sentences, the rest timer speaks, and every control is big enough for a chalky thumb.
Four languages
English, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, in the words people use at the gym.
Your units, your theme
Kilograms or pounds, light or dark. Switch any time; nothing you logged changes.
