Exercises and the plate calculator

The library​

Titan ships with 873 exercises, each with the muscles it works, the equipment it needs and instructions for doing it. The library is part of the app, so it works without a connection like everything else. It comes from the public-domain free-exercise-db project.

Search by name, or open Filters to narrow the list by muscle and equipment: barbell, dumbbell, machine, cable, kettlebell, band, bodyweight and more. Exercises you have done show your best next to their name.

Tap an exercise to see:

  • the muscles it works, drawn on a body diagram: primary in full colour, secondary lighter;
  • its type, equipment, mechanic and difficulty;
  • your next target, if you have done it before;
  • your records and recent attempts;
  • a note of your own: a seat setting, a cue, which machine at your gym.

Your own exercises​

Missing something? Tap New in the library, give it a name, pick its equipment and the muscles it works, and it behaves like any other exercise. You can have the same name twice with different equipment, such as a dumbbell and a cable version.

You can edit or delete your own exercises. An exercise can only be deleted while no logged workout or template uses it, so your history never loses a row.

The plate calculator​

Open it from any exercise's page. Type the weight you want on the bar and the calculator shows which plates go on each side, drawn on a barbell. Open Bar & plates to tell it what your gym actually has: the empty bar's weight and how many pairs of each plate are on the rack. If the rack cannot make the exact weight, it tells you the closest it can load and how far short that is.

The bar weight is also a setting (Settings › Plate calculator bar), and suggested loads use your rack's smallest step, so a target is always a weight you can actually put on the bar.
